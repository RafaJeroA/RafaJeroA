My projects cover quantitative research in macroeconomics, systematic strategies, derivatives, energy and computational modelling.

Based in Spain · EU citizen

## Selected projects

### [Options Analytics Workstation](https://github.com/RafaJeroA/options-analytics-workstation)

Local options application with Black-Scholes pricing and Greeks, implied-volatility solving, exact multi-leg payoff analysis, scenario grids, synthetic market data and experimental read-only IBKR support.

### [News Sentiment Trading Research](https://github.com/RafaJeroA/news-sentiment-trading-algo)

Walk-forward study of equity news-sentiment signals with point-in-time features, training-only parameter selection, explicit execution timing, transaction costs and leakage checks.

### [Spain GDP Nowcast](https://github.com/RafaJeroA/spain-gdp-nowcast-dfm)

A 29-state mixed-frequency dynamic factor model in MATLAB using Kalman filtering and pseudo-out-of-sample evaluation against persistence and AR benchmarks.

### [Bulgaria Euro-Area Accession](https://github.com/RafaJeroA/Bulgaria-Euro-SCM)

Synthetic Control and Local Projection Difference-in-Differences analysis of sovereign yields and inflation around Bulgaria's euro-area accession.

### [Spanish Energy Transition ABM](https://github.com/RafaJeroA/spanish-energy-transition-abm)

Julia agent-based model combining energy dispatch optimisation, investment and financing decisions, and policy scenarios.
